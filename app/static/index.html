Every jacket and pant on this counter is checked for photo coverage and damage disclosure. The record documents what is visible and what the rider reports. It does not test or certify protection. Three shop goals stay in front of the decision.

  1. 01

    Rider awareness

    Age, wash history, labels, and visible wear stay on the record so old gear is not quietly kept in rotation.

  2. 02

    Materials in circulation

    A decline is not a dead end. Leather can become goods, zippers can be salvaged, and textiles can be recycled.

  3. 03

    The in-store conversation

    When buy-in is the wrong call, the same visit opens a trade-in talk toward new riding gear.
